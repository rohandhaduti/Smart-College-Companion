About the Project

Built by students, for students

SmartCampus started as a final-year project at NIT Trichy and grew into a full-fledged platform adopted by universities across India. We believe every student deserves a centralized space to manage their academic journey.



From tracking assignments and exam schedules to discovering clubs and connecting with mentors — SmartCampus is the one platform that truly understands campus life. No more juggling between 5 different apps.

🚀 Our Mission

To simplify and enrich the college experience by giving students a single, intelligent platform that adapts to their unique academic path and goals.
